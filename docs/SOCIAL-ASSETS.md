These assets identify a company's real social accounts; the presence of a logo does not imply endorsement. `wearesocial.com` and `learn.meltwater.com` in the revision list are citation domains, not additional social platforms.

## Facebook, LINE, and LinkedIn: Font Awesome

LinkedIn is absent from this Simple Icons version. The installed **Font Awesome Free Brands 7.3.1** provides `faFacebook`, `faLine`, and `faLinkedin`, retained from the site's existing renderer under the same `facebook`, `line`, and `linkedin` names. Icon artwork: **CC BY 4.0**, Fonticons, Inc., https://fontawesome.com. The original license and attribution are distributed at [`/social-brand-assets/FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt`](../public/social-brand-assets/FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt). Brand references: https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo , https://line.me/en/logo , and https://brand.linkedin.com/downloads . The paths are not redrawn.

## Douyin: DouyinFE Semi Design

`public/social-brand-assets/douyin.svg` is copied without changes from **DouyinFE's Semi Design** musical-note brand asset, named `tiktok_logo.svg` by that upstream library. It is the authentic shared musical-note identity, not an invented icon or text monogram. Its source is explicit so future maintainers can distinguish the upstream filename from the contact platform label.

- Original source: https://github.com/DouyinFE/semi-design/blob/main/packages/semi-icons/src/svgs/tiktok_logo.svg
- Downloaded source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DouyinFE/semi-design/main/packages/semi-icons/src/svgs/tiktok_logo.svg
- Also verified against the `@douyinfe/semi-icons` **2.105.0** npm package, `package/src/svgs/tiktok_logo.svg` (package git head `d0b3e3f034b99eaf8d688efbff9e62c78f4890ce`).
- License: **MIT**, copyright (c) 2021 DouyinFE. The original copyright notice and license are distributed at [`/social-brand-assets/DOUYINFE-MIT-LICENSE.txt`](../public/social-brand-assets/DOUYINFE-MIT-LICENSE.txt).
- Local file SHA-256: `c510592fe924d8cf7ea6d33fce24a1f89ab7c9b7ae64e78de2b4de4038b0d4e3`.

The official `douyin.com` site and its ByteGoofy CDN were inaccessible under this environment's network policy during verification. This record therefore identifies the first-party DouyinFE open-source library as the artwork source; it does not claim a download from the Douyin marketing website or a separate trademark license. Both TikTok and Douyin remain separate platform choices and do not share account links or handles.

The editor may display the monochrome paths using the configured channel color or let an administrator upload approved artwork. Do not describe custom colors, custom backgrounds, or uploaded replacements as official brand variants. A new channel starts with an empty destination so a placeholder account is never published.
